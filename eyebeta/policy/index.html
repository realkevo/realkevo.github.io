Privacy Policy

Eye Offline AI Image Scanner

Effective date: October 14, 2026
Last updated: October 14, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Eye Offline AI Image Scanner ("Eye", "the app", "we", or "our") handles information when you use the application.

1. Overview

Eye is designed to perform its core AI-powered image search and text extraction features locally on your Android device. Your personal images do not need to be uploaded to a remote image-search or OCR server for these core features. Third-party advertising services, where included, may independently process device and advertising information.

Our goal is to provide convenient image discovery and text recognition while minimizing the need to transmit personal image content over the internet.

2. Information Processed by Eye

Depending on the features you use and the permissions you grant, Eye may process the following information locally:

  • Images and media: Images you select as reference images and pictures available to Eye for gallery indexing and visual search.
  • Image analysis data: Locally generated image features, embeddings, image identifiers, and database records used to find visually similar pictures.
  • Extracted text: Text recognized from images when you use Eye's optical character recognition (OCR) feature, including batch extraction from multiple images.
  • Application settings: Preferences such as included or excluded folders, scanning options, and scheduled scanning settings.
  • Scan information: Local indexing records and scan status information needed to maintain and update your image collection.

This information may be stored in the app's local database or private application storage to support its features. The precise information stored depends on your app version and the features you use.

3. Offline AI Image Search

Eye helps you find visually similar pictures within your device's indexed image collection. You can select a reference image, and the app compares its visual characteristics with indexed images to identify potential matches.

The core visual search process is designed to run locally. Eye is not a general internet reverse-image search engine and does not search every image published on the web. Results depend on the images indexed on your device and the capabilities of your installed version.

4. Offline Text Extraction (OCR)

Eye includes text recognition functionality that can extract readable text from images. You can process multiple images in one operation rather than manually handling each image separately.

This feature can be useful for extracting text from screenshots, photographed documents, notes, receipts, signs, and other images containing readable text.

Text recognition is designed to work locally on your device without uploading images to a remote OCR service. Results may vary depending on image quality, language support, text clarity, and other conditions.

5. Permissions and Gallery Access

Eye may request photo, image, or media permissions to identify available pictures, create an image index, and perform visual searches.

Access is used to provide the app's image-search and text-extraction functionality. Eye does not use gallery permission as authorization to publish your personal images.

You can manage or revoke permissions through Android Settings. Restricting access may limit the images Eye can index or search.

6. Background and Scheduled Scanning

Where supported and enabled, Eye may perform background or scheduled scans to update its local image index. Android may require background execution permissions, scheduling permissions, or a foreground-service notification.

Background scanning is intended to maintain the app's image index and support its scanning features. You can adjust available scanning settings in Eye and Android system settings.

7. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Certain versions of Eye may include third-party advertising services, such as Unity Ads, Start.io, or other advertising providers enabled in the installed version.

Depending on the providers and SDKs actually included, these services may collect or process information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and advertising preferences.
  • IP address and approximate location inferred from it.
  • Device model, operating system, and app information.
  • Ad impressions, interactions, and reward events.
  • Fraud-prevention, performance, and diagnostic information.

These services may communicate with their own servers, even while Eye's core image-search and OCR features operate locally. Third-party data handling is governed by the relevant provider's privacy policy.

You can review the following policies where applicable to the advertising SDKs included in your version:

  • Unity Privacy Policy
  • Start.io Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Only the providers actually integrated into your installed version should be considered applicable.

8. Internet Connectivity and Data Sharing

Eye's core visual search and OCR features are designed to work offline. Internet access may be used by integrated advertising SDKs or other online features explicitly provided by the installed version.

We do not sell your personal images or the text extracted from them. Eye's core local search and OCR features are not intended to upload image files or extracted text to our own remote server.

If you choose to share or export content using Android sharing features, the receiving application or service will handle that content according to its own policies.

9. Data Storage and Retention

Eye may retain its local image index, extracted text, scan records, and settings on your device for as long as needed to provide the app's features.

You can clear Eye's application storage or uninstall the app to remove its local app data. This may delete your image index, settings, and other locally stored information. Files saved outside the app's private storage may need to be removed separately.

Third-party advertising providers may retain information they collect according to their own privacy policies and retention practices.

10. Data Security

Eye is designed to keep its core image analysis and search index on your device. We recommend keeping Android updated, protecting your device with a secure screen lock, and installing the application from sources you trust.

No software or storage method can guarantee complete security in every situation.

11. Children's Privacy

Eye is a general-purpose image utility and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children through a service operated by us.

Third-party advertising providers may have their own age-related policies and requirements. The applicable requirements depend on the advertising services and app distribution settings used for the installed version.

12. Your Choices and Controls

You can manage your use of Eye in several ways:

  • Grant or revoke photo and media permissions in Android Settings.
  • Configure available image scanning and folder-selection settings within Eye.
  • Disable scheduled scanning or background features where controls are available.
  • Clear the app's local storage or uninstall Eye to remove its local application data.
  • Review Android's advertising privacy controls and the privacy choices provided by applicable ad networks.

13. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when Eye's features, permissions, integrated third-party services, or data practices change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Please review this page periodically to remain informed about the app's privacy practices.
