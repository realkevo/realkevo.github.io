Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Eye Offline AI Image Scanner, developed by Toptechdevelops, handles information when you install or use the application.

Eye is designed with an offline-first approach. Its core image matching, image scanning, gallery organization and text extraction features are processed locally on your Android device.

Some supporting services, including advertising, purchases and application updates, may require an internet connection and involve third-party service providers.

Images and Photos

Eye accesses images on your device according to the permissions you grant. Images selected for scanning, visual matching or gallery organization are processed locally on your device.

Eye's core image analysis functionality does not require uploading your personal gallery images to Toptechdevelops or an external image-processing server.

Image embeddings, reference images and scanning results used by the application's local matching functionality are stored and processed on your device.

Images and their extracted content are not provided to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

OCR and Text Extraction

Eye provides optical character recognition (OCR) to extract text from supported images.

OCR processing is performed on-device. Extracted text is used to provide the application's text extraction functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to advertising providers.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Eye may display advertisements to support the continued development and maintenance of the application.

We use Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements within the app.

In connection with advertising, Google AdMob and its associated technologies may collect or process certain information, depending on your device, advertising configuration, consent choices and applicable settings.

Such information may include:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers.
  • IP address and associated technical information.
  • Device information, such as device model and operating system.
  • Advertising interactions and ad performance information.
  • Information used for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and related purposes.

This information may be collected or shared by Google and its advertising partners in accordance with their applicable privacy policies and the advertising configuration used by the app.

Advertising personalization and data processing may depend on your location, applicable consent requirements and available privacy controls.

Eye's image scanning, visual matching, OCR and gallery processing features do not depend on advertising data. Your personal gallery images, reference images and extracted OCR text are not intentionally sent to AdMob to serve advertisements.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

You can learn more about how Google uses information from apps that use its advertising services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Internet Connectivity

Eye's core image scanning, visual matching and OCR functionality is designed to work offline using on-device processing.

An internet connection may be required for advertisements, subscription verification, purchases, application updates and other connected services.

Advertising availability may depend on network connectivity. If advertisements cannot be loaded, the core offline functionality is designed to remain independent of advertising availability.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Eye may offer optional premium features and subscriptions. Purchases are processed through Google Play Billing.

Payment credentials and payment transactions are handled by Google Play. Toptechdevelops does not directly collect or store your complete payment card details.

Limited purchase and subscription information may be processed to verify entitlements, manage subscriptions and provide premium functionality.

Google's privacy policy applies to information handled through Google Play services.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features and services you use, Eye and its third-party service providers may process limited information necessary to operate the application.

This may include:

  • Device and technical information required for app operation.
  • Advertising identifiers and advertising-related information.
  • Purchase and subscription verification information.
  • Information necessary for security, diagnostics and service reliability.

Information collected by third-party services is handled according to their respective privacy policies.

Data Storage and Security

Eye's core image processing data, including locally generated image embeddings and scanning information, is stored on the user's device.

We do not operate a cloud image-processing service for Eye's core image matching or OCR functionality.

Third-party services such as Google AdMob and Google Play may independently process information on their own infrastructure under their respective security and privacy practices.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application information remains on your device until it is deleted through the application's available controls, where supported, or by clearing application data or uninstalling Eye.

Information processed by Google AdMob and Google Play is subject to Google's applicable retention and deletion policies.

For information about managing Google account data and advertising privacy settings, consult Google's privacy resources.

Third-Party Services

Eye may integrate third-party services to provide advertising, purchases, subscription verification and other supporting functionality.

These providers may independently collect and process information as necessary to provide their services. Their practices are governed by their respective privacy policies.

Relevant policies include:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Advertising Policies and Technologies

Children's Privacy

Eye is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Advertising and other third-party services may apply additional restrictions and safeguards based on applicable age requirements and consent settings.

Your Privacy Choices

You can manage available advertising privacy settings through your Android device and Google account. These settings may affect how advertisements are personalized and how advertising identifiers are used.

You may also control Eye's access to photos and other device permissions through Android system settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Eye's functionality, third-party integrations or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page. The date shown at the top indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
