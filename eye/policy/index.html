Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Eye Offline AI Image Scanner, developed by Toptechdevelops, handles information when you install or use the application.

Eye is designed with an offline-first approach. Its core image matching, image scanning, gallery organization and text extraction features are processed locally on your Android device.

Eye does not require an account to use its core features. Some supporting services, including advertising, consent management, Google Play purchases and application updates, require an internet connection and may involve third-party service providers.

Images and Photos

Eye accesses images on your device according to the permissions you grant. Images selected for scanning, visual matching or gallery organization are processed locally on your device.

Eye's core image analysis functionality does not require uploading your personal gallery images to Toptechdevelops or an external image-processing server.

Image embeddings, reference images and scanning results used by the application's local matching functionality are stored and processed on your device.

Images and their extracted content are not provided to Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

OCR and Text Extraction

Eye provides optical character recognition (OCR) to extract text from supported images.

OCR processing is performed on-device. Extracted text is used to provide the application's text extraction functionality and is not intentionally transmitted to advertising providers.

Advertising and Google AdMob

Eye may display advertisements to support the continued development and maintenance of the application. We use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob), an advertising service provided by Google, to request and display advertisements.

When the advertising SDK operates, Google and, where applicable, advertising partners may automatically collect or receive information from the app or device. The data involved can depend on the device, SDK configuration, advertising request, consent choices and applicable law.

Depending on those circumstances, advertising-related information may include:

  • IP address, which may be used to estimate approximate location.
  • Advertising ID, App Set ID and other device or advertising identifiers.
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, ad interactions and video views.
  • Diagnostics and performance information, such as app or SDK performance measurements.
  • Device and technical information, such as device model, operating system and network information.

This information may be collected and shared with Google and applicable advertising partners for purposes such as ad delivery, advertising measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security and, where permitted, advertising personalisation. The specific processing depends on the advertising services in use and the user's privacy choices.

Approximate location may be inferred from an IP address. Eye's image-scanning and OCR features do not require precise GPS location, and Eye does not send gallery images, reference images, image embeddings or extracted OCR text to AdMob for advertising purposes.

Eye's image scanning, visual matching, OCR and gallery processing features do not depend on advertising data. Those core features are designed to remain independent of advertising availability.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

You can also learn how Google uses information from apps that use its services here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services .

Internet Connectivity

Eye's core image scanning, visual matching, gallery organization and OCR functionality is designed to work offline using on-device processing.

An internet connection is required for services such as loading advertisements, communicating with Google AdMob and its consent service, completing or refreshing Google Play purchases, and checking for application updates.

Advertising availability depends on network connectivity, consent status, ad eligibility and inventory. If an ad cannot be loaded, the core offline image-processing functionality is designed to remain available.

Purchases and Subscriptions

Eye may offer optional premium features and subscriptions. Purchases are initiated and processed through Google Play Billing. Google Play handles payment processing and payment credentials. Toptechdevelops does not receive or store your complete payment card or bank account details.

Eye receives purchase and subscription status information through Google Play Billing so that it can determine whether Premium features should be unlocked. In the current app implementation, Premium entitlement information, such as the unlocked status, selected plan identifier and purchase time, is stored locally on your device.

Eye does not send purchase tokens or purchase receipts to a Toptechdevelops-operated payment-verification server. Google Play processes the transaction and handles transaction information under Google's own terms and privacy practices.

For information about data handled by Google Play, review the Google Privacy Policy and the terms shown during purchase.

Information We May Process

Depending on the features and services you use, information may be processed locally by Eye or by third-party services that support advertising, consent management, purchases and application updates.

Examples include:

  • Images, reference images, image embeddings, OCR text and scan results processed and stored locally on your device.
  • Advertising identifiers, IP-address-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics processed by Google AdMob.
  • Consent and privacy choices managed through Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP).
  • Purchase status and Premium entitlement information received through Google Play Billing and stored locally by Eye.
  • Technical information needed to maintain application updates, security and reliability.

Information processed by third-party services is handled according to their respective privacy policies and the settings or choices available for those services.

Data Storage and Security

Eye's core image-processing data, including locally generated image embeddings, reference-image information, OCR results and scanning information, is stored on the user's device.

We do not operate a cloud image-processing service for Eye's core image matching or OCR functionality. Eye does not upload users' personal gallery images or extracted OCR text to a Toptechdevelops image-processing server.

Google AdMob and other Google services may independently transmit and process the advertising, diagnostic, device and consent-related information described in this policy on Google's infrastructure and, where applicable, with its advertising partners.

Data transmitted by Google services is subject to their security practices, including encryption in transit where supported. Review Google's privacy and security information for details about its services.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored application information remains on your device until it is removed through the application's available controls, where supported, or by clearing Eye's app storage or uninstalling the application. Uninstalling Eye may not remove information that you separately saved outside the app.

Eye does not create user accounts and does not operate a developer-hosted cloud store for users' gallery images, OCR text or local scan indexes. Eye currently does not provide a separate account-deletion or in-app request mechanism for deleting data held on a Toptechdevelops server.

Information collected or processed by Google AdMob, Google UMP and Google Play is subject to Google's applicable retention, privacy and deletion policies. Users can review Google's privacy resources and account controls for information about data held by Google.

Third-Party Services

Eye uses third-party services for advertising, consent management and Google Play purchases. These services may collect or process information as described in this policy and in their own privacy documentation.

  • Google Privacy Policy — covers Google services including Google Play.
  • Google Advertising Technologies — information about Google's advertising technologies.
  • Google AdMob and publisher privacy information — information for users and publishers about advertising data.

Children's Privacy

Eye is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Advertising and other third-party services may apply additional restrictions and safeguards based on applicable age requirements and consent settings.

Your Privacy Choices

In regions where consent messages are required, Eye uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present available advertising consent and privacy choices. The message may allow you to accept, reject or manage the purposes and advertising partners described in the message.

Your choices can affect whether personalised advertising or other types of ads are eligible to be shown. Rejecting optional advertising purposes does not prevent you from using Eye's core offline image scanning, matching and OCR features. Ad availability may nevertheless be limited or unavailable depending on your choices and applicable rules.

Where the UMP privacy-options feature is available, you can reopen the consent choices through the Privacy Options control in Eye's settings. You can also manage relevant advertising settings through your Android device and Google account. The controls available may depend on your region, device and applicable requirements.

You can control Eye's access to photos and other device permissions through Android system settings.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Eye's functionality, third-party integrations or applicable legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this page. The date shown at the top indicates when the policy was most recently updated.
