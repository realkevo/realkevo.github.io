Eye FAQ

Frequently asked questions about Eye Offline AI Image Scanner for Android.

What is Eye Offline AI Image Scanner?

Eye is an Android application that uses AI-powered image analysis to help users search image collections and find visually similar images.

Does Eye work offline?

Eye is designed with offline image processing capabilities, allowing important image analysis to take place directly on the Android device without requiring a constant internet connection.

Can Eye find similar images?

Yes. Eye allows users to provide a reference image and scan image collections for visually similar images.

Can Eye extract text from images?

Yes. Eye includes OCR capabilities for extracting text from images.

Does Eye require an internet connection to scan images?

Eye is designed for offline image scanning and on-device processing. Some services or features outside the core offline scanning functionality may require connectivity.

Where can I download Eye?

Eye Offline AI Image Scanner is available through Google Play.
