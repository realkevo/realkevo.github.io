Eye Offline AI Image Scanner

Eye is an offline AI-powered image scanner for Android designed to help you search image collections, find visually similar images, and extract information from images directly on your device.

Download Eye on Google Play

What is Eye?

Eye turns an image into a visual search query. Instead of manually searching through thousands of photographs, you can provide a reference image and let the application scan your device for visually similar images.

Eye is designed around on-device processing, allowing image analysis to be performed without requiring a constant internet connection.

Features

Offline AI

Perform image analysis on your Android device without depending on a continuous internet connection.

Visual Image Matching

Search image collections and identify images that are visually similar to a reference image.

Large Image Collections

Scan large collections of images to help locate visually relevant photographs.

OCR

Extract text from images using optical character recognition capabilities.

Android

Built specifically for Android devices with a focus on practical mobile image searching.

Privacy Focused

Eye is designed to perform important image processing directly on the user's device.

How Eye Works

First, select an image to use as a reference. Eye analyzes the visual characteristics of that image and then searches your selected image collection for visually similar images.

The application can also use OCR to extract text from images, providing another way to find information within your photo collection.
