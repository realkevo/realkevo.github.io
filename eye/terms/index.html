2. Description of the App

Eye is an Android application designed to help users search, identify and organise images on their devices.

Its features include:

  • Offline image similarity matching.
  • Optical Character Recognition (OCR) for extracting text from images.
  • Local image indexing and scanning.
  • Image folder inclusion and exclusion controls.
  • Background and scheduled scanning features.
  • Additional functionality available through Eye Premium.

Eye is designed to perform its image scanning and matching functionality locally on your device without requiring you to upload your personal gallery images to our servers.
