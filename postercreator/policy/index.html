Privacy Policy

Creator - Offline Poster Editor & Video Maker

Effective date: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Creator ("the App"), developed and published by Toptech Develops ("we", "our", or "us"), handles information when you use the application.

Privacy first: Creator is designed to work offline. Your photos, videos, text, saved drafts, and exported designs are processed and stored locally on your device. The App does not require an account or a cloud service to perform its core editing and export functions.

1. Information We Collect

Creator does not operate its own server to collect or receive your personal information. The core editing functionality does not require you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or account credentials.

The App may access or process the following information locally:

  • Photos and images: Images you select for use in your posters.
  • Videos and audio: Video files you import for placement on your canvas and audio tracks used during video export.
  • Text and design content: Captions, text colors, font sizes, element positions, dimensions, and layer ordering.
  • Saved projects: Local project metadata, draft information, file references, and export details where these features are supported.
  • Exported files: PNG images and MP4 videos generated when you export a design.

This information is used to provide the App's editing, preview, project-saving, and export features. It is not transmitted to us as part of normal offline operation.

2. Photos, Videos, and Device Storage

Creator accesses media that you choose to import using the available Android media selection interface or other necessary storage mechanisms.

Imported media may be copied into app-managed local storage so that your projects can continue to work if the original files are moved or removed. The App may also maintain local references to media files, depending on its implementation.

Creator processes media locally to display your design, render text and images, compose video frames, and generate exported files. The App is designed not to upload your imported photos or videos to a remote server.

3. Offline Operation and Internet Access

Creator's core editing, project management, and export functions are designed to operate without an internet connection. No online account, cloud storage, or remote processing service is required for these functions.

Where the published version does not integrate advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or other network-dependent SDKs, the App does not use those services to transmit usage data. If such services are introduced in a future version, this policy will be updated to describe their data practices.

4. Local Projects and Drafts

If you save a project, its design information and associated media references are stored locally on your device. Depending on the features implemented in your version, project information may be maintained in a local SQLite database.

Creator does not automatically synchronize drafts with a remote account or cloud service.

You are responsible for maintaining backups of important projects and exported files. Uninstalling the App, clearing its storage, or deleting its files may remove locally stored projects and media.

5. Exported Images and Videos

Creator generates PNG images and MP4 videos from the content you arrange on the canvas. Video composition, frame rendering, and encoding are performed locally on the device.

Depending on your Android version and the App's storage implementation, exported files may be saved to a user-accessible media location or another local directory. MediaScannerConnection may be used to make exported files visible in the device's gallery.

You control whether you share or distribute exported files. If you choose to share a file through another application, that application's privacy policy and data-handling practices will apply to its subsequent processing.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

Creator does not sell your personal information. The App's core functionality does not send your projects, imported media, captions, or exported designs to us or share them with advertising networks.

Your files may be shared with other applications when you explicitly use Android's sharing interface. Any information you independently disclose to another service is subject to that service's policies.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Project data and media remain on your device until you delete them, remove the relevant files, clear the App's storage, or uninstall the App, subject to Android's storage behavior and any files saved outside the App's private directory.

You can delete saved projects and exported files using the controls provided by the App or the appropriate Android file-management tools.

Creator does not maintain a remote copy of your local projects through its core offline functionality. Therefore, we do not have a server-side project database from which to retrieve or delete those files.

8. Data Security

Keeping editing and export operations on your device reduces the need to transmit your media over the internet. However, no storage method or software application can guarantee absolute security.

The security of locally stored files also depends on your device's security settings, Android version, and the permissions granted to other applications.

9. Children's Privacy

Creator is a general-purpose creative tool and is not specifically designed to collect personal information from children. The App's core offline functionality does not require users to create accounts or submit personal information to us.

If the App's data practices change in a way that affects children's information, this policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Your Privacy Choices

You can exercise control over your content by:

  • Selecting only the photos and videos you want to import.
  • Deleting drafts and exported files you no longer need.
  • Managing the App's permissions in Android settings.
  • Choosing whether to share an exported file with another app.
  • Uninstalling Creator if you no longer wish to use it, while remembering that files saved outside its private storage may need to be deleted separately.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's features, technical implementation, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any revised policy will be published at the location where this policy is hosted. The effective date at the top of the page will be updated when appropriate.

12. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Creator's privacy practices, contact the developer:

  • Developer: Toptech Develops
  • Contact: https://github.com/realkevo

Please use the developer contact page to obtain the appropriate contact channel for privacy-related enquiries.
