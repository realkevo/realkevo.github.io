Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 21, 2026

Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Eye Offline AI Image Scanner, developed by Toptechdevelops, handles information when you use the application.

Images and Photos

Eye is designed to perform its core image scanning and visual matching functionality on the user's Android device.

Images selected for scanning may be processed locally on the device as part of the application's image analysis functionality.

OCR and Text Extraction

When OCR functionality is used, text may be extracted from images on the device as part of the application's features.

Internet Connectivity

The core image scanning functionality is designed to support offline operation. However, certain features such as advertising, application updates, purchases, or external services may require an internet connection.

Advertising

Eye may display advertisements through third-party advertising services. These services may collect information according to their own privacy policies.

Purchases

Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed through Google Play Billing. Payment information is handled by Google Play and is not directly collected by Toptechdevelops.

Information We May Receive

Depending on the features you use, the application or associated services may process limited technical or transactional information necessary to provide the service, verify purchases, prevent abuse, or maintain the application.

Children's Privacy

Eye is not specifically directed toward children under the applicable minimum age for digital services. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.
