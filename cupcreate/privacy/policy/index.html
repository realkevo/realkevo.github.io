Privacy Policy – CupCreate – Poster Maker

Toptech Develops Limited respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how CupCreate – Poster Maker handles information when you use our Android application.

CupCreate is an offline-first poster design and video creation application. Its core creative features are designed to work locally on your device. You can create posters, add text and images, arrange design elements, and export your work without uploading your creative projects to our servers.

By using CupCreate, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information

CupCreate does not require you to create an account, register, log in, or provide personal profile information to use its core features.

We do not operate a developer account database and do not collect your name, phone number, or email address through the App's creative functionality.

1.2 Advertising Information

CupCreate may display advertisements provided by third-party advertising partners, including Google AdMob.

These advertising providers may automatically collect or process certain information when advertisements are displayed or interacted with. Depending on your device settings, location, applicable consent requirements, and advertising configuration, this information may include:

  • Android Advertising ID (GAID).
  • IP address.
  • Device model and general device information.
  • Android operating system version.
  • Advertising impressions, clicks, and interaction information.
  • Advertising performance, diagnostics, and fraud-prevention information.

Advertising partners may use this information to provide and measure advertisements, prevent fraudulent activity, improve advertising services, and, where permitted, deliver personalized or contextual advertising.

We do not use your poster designs, imported photos, videos, or creative projects as advertising content, and we do not upload those files to our servers.

For more information about Google's data practices, visit: Google Privacy Policy .

2. How We Use Information

Information processed by advertising providers may be used for:

  • Displaying advertisements within the App.
  • Measuring advertisement performance.
  • Preventing invalid advertising activity and fraud.
  • Improving advertising delivery and reliability.
  • Providing personalized or non-personalized advertisements, as applicable.

The App's core poster creation, editing, and export functionality operates locally on your device.

3. Photos, Videos, and Storage Permissions

CupCreate may request access to photos and videos on your device so you can import media into your designs.

Depending on your Android version, the App may use permissions such as:

  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES
  • READ_MEDIA_VIDEO
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on supported older Android versions.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE on supported older Android versions.

These permissions allow you to select and use your own media in poster designs and video projects.

Your imported photos and videos are not uploaded to Toptech Develops Limited's servers. Your creative projects and drafts are stored locally on your device.

You can manage the App's media permissions through your Android device settings.

4. Local Storage and Creative Projects

CupCreate is designed to process your creative content on your device.

The App may use local application storage and an on-device SQLite database to store information such as:

  • Poster projects and drafts.
  • Design elements and text.
  • References to imported media.
  • Project settings and editing information.
  • Other data required to preserve your work.

Poster rendering, image exporting, and video compilation are performed locally using the device's available processing capabilities.

We do not collect, upload, store, or sell your poster designs, imported photos, videos, or project files through developer-operated servers.

Your locally stored projects may be lost if you uninstall the App, clear its application data, or lose access to the device without having made your own backup.

5. Internet and Network Permissions

CupCreate may request the following Android permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE

These permissions support internet connectivity and network-state checks, including the operation of third-party advertising services.

An internet connection may be required for advertisements to load. The App's core creative functionality is designed to operate offline.

6. Sharing and Exporting Content

CupCreate allows you to export your creations, including supported image and video formats.

You may also choose to share exported content through Android's standard sharing interface.

Sharing is initiated by you. When you select another application or service to receive your exported content, that service's own privacy policy and data-handling practices apply.

Toptech Develops Limited does not receive a copy of your exported content through this sharing process.

7. Third-Party Services

CupCreate may integrate third-party services, particularly advertising services such as Google AdMob.

These services operate under their own privacy policies and may process information according to their respective terms.

We encourage you to review the relevant policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Advertising Information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

We do not control how independent third-party providers process information under their own policies.

8. Advertising Preferences and Your Choices

You may be able to manage or reset your Android Advertising ID and adjust advertising privacy preferences through your device settings.

On many Android devices, these options can be found under:

Settings → Google → Ads

The exact menu location and available options may vary depending on your Android version and device manufacturer.

Where required, applicable consent choices and advertising preferences may also be presented through the advertising services integrated into the App.

Changing advertising preferences does not necessarily remove advertisements. It may affect whether advertisements are personalized.

9. Children's Privacy

CupCreate is not intended to knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

Where the App is made available to children or included in a family-oriented distribution, applicable advertising and data-handling configurations are intended to follow relevant Google Play Families requirements and applicable children's privacy laws.

Advertising behavior depends on the App's actual audience settings, advertising SDK configuration, applicable consent requirements, and the services used.

Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the App and contact us if they have concerns about privacy.

10. Data Security and Retention

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App.

Creative projects and imported media are stored locally on your device. We do not maintain developer-operated cloud storage for your creative content.

Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to their respective retention and security practices.

You can remove locally stored App data by clearing the App's data or uninstalling it. This may permanently delete your saved projects and drafts.

11. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location and applicable laws, you may have rights relating to personal information processed by third-party services, including rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict certain processing.

For advertising-related information, you may need to exercise these rights directly with the relevant advertising provider.

You can also manage available advertising preferences through your Android device settings.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when CupCreate's functionality, advertising integrations, data practices, or applicable legal requirements change.

Any revised policy will be published at:

https://realkevo.github.io/cupcreate/privacy/policy/

The updated effective date will appear at the beginning of the policy.

We encourage users to review this page periodically.

13. Our Privacy Commitment

CupCreate is built around local creative work and user control.

Our core commitments are:

  • Your poster designs and drafts remain on your device.
  • Your imported photos and videos are not uploaded to our servers.
  • Core creative functionality is designed to work offline.
  • You do not need an account to use the App's core features.
  • Third-party advertising providers may process advertising-related information as explained in this policy.

Toptech Develops Limited is committed to being transparent about how CupCreate handles information and to respecting users' privacy.
